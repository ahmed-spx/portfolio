Who am I?

I am a software engineering student and aspiring developer with experience building full-stack applications, improving technical workflows, and leveraging emerging technologies to solve real-world problems. Over the past several years, my work has focused on developing scalable software solutions, exploring AI-driven development practices, and creating applications that improve efficiency and accessibility for users.

Through academic projects, internships, hackathons, and collaborative experiences, I have strengthened my passion for building technology that creates meaningful impact beyond the code itself. I have built and deployed full-stack applications using Java Spring Boot, React, Node.js, Docker, and Kubernetes. I also enjoy exploring how artificial intelligence can accelerate development, using tools such as GitHub Copilot, Google AI Studio, Google's Antigravity IDE, OpenAI Codex and Claude Code to streamline software delivery.

I approach my work with curiosity, collaboration, and a continuous learning mindset. Outside of software engineering, I enjoy strength training, following advancements in technology and artificial intelligence, and connecting with professionals who are passionate about innovation. I am always interested in opportunities to learn, collaborate, and contribute to projects that make a positive difference.
